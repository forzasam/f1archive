Contact

Help make the archive better.

Found an error, have an idea for a feature, or want to discuss a collaboration? There are two ways to get in touch.

Support

Bug reports and suggestions

Use the support address for technical problems, incorrect data, feature requests, usability feedback, or anything else that could improve the site.

support@f1archive.net
For a useful bug report, please include:
  • what you were trying to do
  • what happened instead
  • the relevant page, season or race
  • screenshots or error messages, where available

General enquiries

Collaborations and contributions

Use the general contact address for collaborations, media enquiries, historical contributions, data corrections requiring more context, or any other conversation about the project.

contact@f1archive.net
Suitable for:
  • collaboration proposals
  • editorial or historical contributions
  • media and project enquiries
  • anything not covered by support

F1 Archive is maintained independently, so responses may not always be immediate. Every genuine message is appreciated and will be read.
