Privacy policy

A plain-language explanation of the limited personal information processed by F1 Archive.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Who operates F1 Archive

F1 Archive is an independently maintained informational and editorial website. For the personal information described in this policy, F1 Archive is responsible for deciding why and how that information is used. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to contact@f1archive.net.

Overview

The website does not require user accounts and does not sell personal information. F1 Archive uses limited technical information to operate and secure the site, information voluntarily provided in messages or by contributors, and—only with visitor consent—Google Analytics to understand how the archive is used. Analytics information is not used by F1 Archive for personalised advertising or marketing.

Information collected automatically

When the website is accessed, the hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as an IP address, browser or device information, the requested page, referring information, and the date and time of the request. This information may appear in server or application logs and is used to deliver the website, maintain security, diagnose faults and investigate abuse.

F1 Archive relies on its legitimate interests in operating, securing and maintaining the website for this processing. Hosting and infrastructure providers may retain technical records according to their own operational and security requirements.

Google Analytics

With your consent, F1 Archive uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. Analytics may process information such as pages visited, approximate location, browser and device characteristics, referring source, and interactions with the site. F1 Archive uses this information to measure traffic, identify usability issues and improve the archive.

Google Analytics is provided by Google. For visitors in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Google states that Analytics does not log or store individual IP addresses: IP addresses are used to derive coarse location information and are then discarded before the data is logged. F1 Archive does not use Analytics to identify individual visitors and does not use Analytics data for personalised advertising.

The lawful basis for F1 Archive's use of optional analytics is your consent. If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded by F1 Archive. You can change your analytics choice using the site's cookie controls; withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

Cookies and local storage

F1 Archive asks for consent before loading Google Analytics or allowing optional analytics storage to be used. You can accept or reject analytics through the cookie banner. A strictly necessary preference record may be stored locally so that the site remembers your choice. This preference is used only to operate the consent mechanism.

Other strictly necessary technical storage may be used by the browser or hosting infrastructure where required to deliver, secure or reliably operate the website. Optional analytics is not required to use the archive.

Messages and correspondence

If you contact F1 Archive by email or through a project communication channel, the information you choose to provide—such as your name or username, contact details and the contents of your message—may be used to respond, investigate reports, process contributor enquiries, maintain editorial records and protect the project from misuse.

This information is normally processed because it is necessary for F1 Archive's legitimate interests in communicating with users, administering the project and maintaining accurate editorial records. Correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, including where context needs to be preserved for a correction, contribution, dispute or abuse report.

Contributors and author pages

If you contribute editorial material to F1 Archive, information you provide for publication—such as your chosen author name, biography, profile details and social or external links—may be displayed publicly on the site alongside your work. Information intended for publication will remain public for as long as the relevant contribution or author profile remains part of the archive, unless it is later removed or amended.

Contributor contact details and private editorial correspondence are not published merely because you contribute. Requests to correct or remove profile information can be sent to contact@f1archive.net.

Third-party services and external links

The site is hosted using Render, uses historical motorsport data sourced through Jolpica-F1, and uses Google Analytics for consent-based website usage statistics. Email, Discord and other external services may also process information when you choose to contact F1 Archive or follow an external link through those services. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms and may operate outside the UK.

Some service providers may process information in countries outside the UK. Where F1 Archive is responsible for such a transfer, it relies on the transfer mechanism or safeguards applicable to the relevant provider and service.

How long information is kept

Retention depends on the type of information and why it is needed. Analytics information is retained according to the retention settings configured for the F1 Archive Google Analytics property and Google's applicable retention rules. Technical logs are retained according to the hosting provider's operational settings. Correspondence is kept only while reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received or for related editorial, security or dispute-resolution needs. Public contributor information may remain with the relevant archived work as described above.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights over personal information relating to you, including rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise a right or ask how information about you is being used, email contact@f1archive.net. F1 Archive may need enough information to identify the relevant records and verify a request where appropriate.

You also have the right to raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe your personal information has been handled improperly.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as the site, its services or applicable requirements change. The date at the top will be updated when a material change is made.
